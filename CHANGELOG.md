# Changelog

## [0.1.0] – 2026-11-01

First public release.

- Single-file web interface (HTML/CSS/JavaScript), runs offline.
- Feedstocks: Brazilian sugarcane, first- and second-crop corn (24 state inventories from Pereira et al. 2025), US corn, user-defined ethanol CI.
- Plant configurations: stand-alone corn mill (eucalyptus CHP) and integrated cane–corn mill (bagasse CHP).
- Hydrogen routes: PEM (grid, bagasse, wind), ethanol steam reforming, natural gas SMR; adjustable grid factor, electrolyser consumption and H₂ demand.
- Land-use change: CORSIA ILUC, BRLUC dLUC, consequential (Moreira et al. 2020).
- Outputs: CI, savings vs CORSIA and RED III, threshold flags, hotspot chart and table, benchmark chart, H₂ break-even chart, state comparison table, scenario pinning, CSV export.
